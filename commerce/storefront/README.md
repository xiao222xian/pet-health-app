## Purpose

Static overseas-facing landing page prototype for a pet supplement brand under the broader `pet` workspace. The first version is designed for traffic from TikTok, Meta, Google, or creator content and focuses on direct ecommerce conversion rather than AI app or robot positioning.

## Scope

- English DTC storefront landing page.
- Product positioning for daily pet wellness support.
- Trust blocks: ingredients, testing, reviews, FAQ, guarantee.
- Email capture and buy CTA placeholders.

## Not In Scope

- Checkout backend.
- Inventory or order management.
- Medical, disease-treatment, or veterinary drug claims.
- AI app or pet robot branding in the first customer-facing version.

## Files

- `index.html`: page content and structure.
- `styles.css`: responsive visual system.
- `script.js`: small interactions for FAQ and email capture placeholder.
- `memory/`: AI handoff notes.
