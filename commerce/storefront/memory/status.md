## What This Project Is

Static landing page prototype for a pet supplement ecommerce brand. It is meant to validate product positioning, trust messaging, and conversion flow before connecting checkout, backend, AI app, or robot concepts.

## What Works

- Static project scaffold exists.
- First page should be viewable by opening `index.html`.
- Project now lives under `/Users/admin/developer/pet/commerce/storefront/`.

## What Is Broken Or Unknown

- Brand name, product name, packaging, ingredient list, certificates, supplier claims, price, shipping, refund policy, checkout URL, and legal copy are placeholders.
- No live checkout or analytics.

## Current Risks

- Pet supplement copy can easily become medical or drug-like; avoid claims such as treating diarrhea, curing skin disease, curing tear stains, or treating arthritis.
- Supplier documents and compliance must be checked before real launch.
