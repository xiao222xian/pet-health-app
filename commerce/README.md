# Commerce

Status: active
Last reviewed: 2026-06-17

This directory contains the pet commerce and cash-flow experiments for the broader pet health ecosystem.

The first business line is an overseas-facing pet supplement storefront. It should be treated as a direct ecommerce validation track, not as an AI app or robot feature in the first public test.

## Modules

- `storefront/`: static overseas pet supplement landing page prototype.

## Positioning

Short-term customer-facing positioning:

- pet daily wellness
- supplement ecommerce
- simple ingredients
- trust, reviews, routine, and repeat purchase

Do not lead with AI app, pet robot, or health-data platform concepts in the first storefront test. Those belong to the long-term strategy after ecommerce demand and repeat purchase are validated.

## Compliance Notes

- Avoid disease-treatment claims.
- Avoid wording such as curing diarrhea, curing tear stains, treating skin disease, or treating arthritis.
- Keep claims around daily wellness support, normal digestion support, coat support, and active-life support unless approved labels and legal review allow more.
- Supplier certificates, testing reports, private cost, and factory documents should not be committed unless explicitly sanitized and approved.
